Overview

What is Penguin Kingdom?

Penguin Kingdom is the best. No, this isn't opinion, it's fact. Penguin Kingdpom is comprised of the King and Queen, a Parliament, and a voting system.
In Penguin Land, everyone is happy and sweet all the time! The Queen and King have implemented many programs through Parliament to ensure this. The Penguins in the Kingdom have all the support they possibly could imagine. With things like free schooling, healthcare, and no taxes, the Penguins live in a utopia. As schooling begins for the little penguins and penguinettes, they are taught the main values of Penguin Kingdom;

  • Kindness
  • Empathy
  • Respect
  • Dignity for all Penguin kind

FAQS

Here are the most commonly asked quesetions about Penguin Land;

  1. Do I have to be a Penguin to be a citizen?
    • No! After becoming a citizen you become a penguin!
  2. How long does it take to become a citizen?
    • It takes about 2 and 1/2 years.
  3. What will cause you to get in trouble in Penguin Kingdom?
    • If you are disrespectful, dishonest, and rude to anyone in Penguin Land it will cause you to get in trouble.
      This trouble can lead to fines, arrests, and even being banished from the Penguin Kingdom
